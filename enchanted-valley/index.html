02 · Tree inventory

24,099 Christmas trees, each one located and sized

From the same mapping flights, every tree in 30 blocks across 37 acres was found and measured. Nobody walked a row with a tally counter. The result is an inventory by retail size class, block by block.

About 5,100 trees are in the 6 to 10 ft range this season, and five blocks (S11, S7, S4, M5, and M6) hold about two-thirds of the 6 to 8 footers.

The biggest group is the 7,664 trees at 2 to 4 ft, the stock that carries the next several seasons. The blocks near the visitor area show scattered large trees with open ground between them, the pattern of blocks that have been cut through.

Heights come from the drone surface model, which tends to read conifer tips low, so class counts are estimates until a 30-tree field check with a tape is done. Young seedlings, especially in the east field, are undercounted.

03 · Ridge & hunting ground

Read the wooded ridge the way deer use it

The bare-earth model sees under the full canopy. From it, the terrain features hunters usually spend seasons learning on foot were mapped: where water and cool air flow, the flat resting shelves on steep slopes, and the draws that connect the timber to the fields.

ThermalsCool air sinks down the draws in the morning and rises in the evening. That tells you where your scent goes and which stand to hunt when.
Drainages & drawsThe travel routes between ridge and field, and where seasonal water and wet ground will be.
BenchesFlat shelves on steep slopes where deer bed and travel, easy to miss on foot under full canopy.
Stands & accessPlan stand sites and quiet entry routes from a measured map, with files that load into Google Earth and most hunting apps.

04 · 3D structures

The ridge-top stand, measured in 3D

One automated orbit around the elevated stand on the ridge turned 842 positioned photos into a textured 3D model at true real-world scale. Every stair, brace, and leg can be measured, with no tape, ladder, or climbing. The same flight works for barns, sheds, and any other building on the property.
