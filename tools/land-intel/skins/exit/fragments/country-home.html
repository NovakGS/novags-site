On a Home With Acreage

This sample is vacant wooded land. On a listing with a house, the same two flights put the home on the map too, so a buyer sees the house, the outbuildings, and the land around them in one place before the first showing.

Home and OutbuildingsFootprints of the house, garage, barns, and sheds, with square footage measured from the aerial.
Driveway and AccessDriveway length and grade from the LiDAR, so a buyer can judge a steep climb or a long winter plow.
Field, Woods, and PastureAcres of each cover type. On this parcel that is 30.8 acres of hardwood and 1.7 acres of open ground.
Roof and Wall Thermal CheckOn a listing flown in cold weather, the night thermal flight can also show where the house holds heat and where it leaks, shared only if the seller chooses.
