The Closing Gift: A First-Winter Home Thermal Check

On a listing with a home, New Owner Stewardship starts with a gift from the agent at closing: one thermal flight over the house during the first cold stretch of winter, flown on a calm evening with the heat on inside, so its date depends on the weather. A thermal camera points to where warmth may be leaving the building, so the new owner knows where to have a contractor look before the first full heating bill arrives.

Roof and AtticWarm streaks on the roof that point to thin or missing insulation, attic air leaks, and the spots where ice dams are likely to form.
Walls, Windows, and DoorsSigns of heat loss at window and door frames, the sill and rim joist, and wall sections with gaps in the insulation.
Findings You Can Hand OffEach finding as a thermal image beside a normal photo of the same spot, with a short summary PDF to give a contractor or energy auditor.

Given by the Agent, Then Paid by the Owner

The first-winter check is the agent's closing gift. After that, repeat checks, acreage flights, and wildlife surveys are booked and paid for by the owner, directly with Novak Geospatial Solutions™. Thermal findings are indications for a qualified contractor to verify, not a diagnosis or a home inspection.
