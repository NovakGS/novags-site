Your free aerial map

On the mapping flight, the drone flies a grid over your property and takes hundreds of overlapping photos. We stitch them into one seamless, map-accurate image of the whole property, called an orthomosaic. Think of it as your own current, high-resolution version of Google Earth.

  • Sharp enough to see individual fence posts, rows and paths
  • Useful for planning parking, maze layouts, plantings and drainage
  • Delivered as a download link you can keep and print

Optional add-on

Busy-day aerial photo package

We come back on one of your busy days and photograph the farm the way your guests experience it: full parking, wagons moving, families in the maze. Shots are taken high enough that no one can be identified, so the photos are ready for your website, social media, ads and print.

  • 8–12 finished photos, each one hand-edited for color, exposure, straight horizons and clean cropping
  • A mix of wide views of the whole farm and closer views of your main attractions
  • Full-resolution files for print, plus web-sized copies for social media
  • Delivered within 5 business days by download link
  • The photos are yours, with full rights to use them however you like: website, social media, ads, print and signage
  • Flown by an FAA-certified remote pilot, never directly over guests

The portfolio option, explained

What “portfolio use” means

You still own your photos and keep full rights to them. Choosing the 50% option adds one thing: permission for us to use those same photos in our marketing. Nothing is taken away from you.

We may

  • Show the photos on our website and social media
  • Use them in our brochures and proposals
  • Credit and tag your farm by name, which is free exposure for you

We will never

  • Sell your photos or license them to anyone else
  • Use a photo where a person can be identified
  • Keep up a photo you ask us to take down

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Mapping flight. We fly the practice mapping flight at a time that works for you and send your free aerial map.
  2. Choose your option. If you'd like the photo package, tell us which pricing option you want. No pressure either way.
  3. Pick a busy day. We agree on a day together, such as a fall weekend or a Christmas tree weekend. We launch from a spot you approve, away from guests, and are typically on site about an hour.
  4. Delivery, then payment. Your edited photos arrive within 5 business days. We send an invoice after delivery.

Questions

Common questions

Is the aerial map really free?

Yes. You're giving us a place to practice and refine our mapping work, and the map is our thank-you. There's no obligation to buy anything.

Will my guests notice the drone or end up in the photos?

We fly high and off to the side, never directly over guests. People appear only as small figures that give the scene life; no one can be recognized.

What if the weather is bad on our busy day?

Safety comes first, so we won't fly in high wind, rain or poor visibility. We'll simply pick another busy day at no extra cost.

Who owns the photos?

You do. The finished photos are yours, with full rights to use, edit, publish and print them however you like. With the 50% option, we keep only a permission to use them in our own marketing. With the other options, we don't use them at all.

Can I switch options later?

Yes. If you choose Founding client and later decide you're happy for us to feature the photos, just tell us and we'll refund the $100 difference.
