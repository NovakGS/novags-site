More Thermal Work

Buildings & Energy

Roof Moisture ScansWet insulation under a flat roof holds the day's heat after sunset. A dusk flight outlines the areas for a roofer to check. Solar Panel ScansHot cells, failed diodes, and strings that have dropped offline show as warm spots while the array is producing. Heat Loss SurveysIn cold weather, missing insulation and air leaks show as warm patches on walls, parapets, and around openings. Rooftop HVAC ChecksRooftop units scanned for parts running hotter than the rest, without sending anyone up. Ice Dam & Roof Heat LossWarm streaks on a winter roof show where heat is escaping and where ice dams are likely to form. Snowmelt System ChecksHeated driveways, walks, and ramps scanned to show which loops are working and where one has gone cold.

Farm & Land

Irrigation Leak ChecksWet soil runs cooler than dry soil, so leaks and overwatered zones stand out across a field or turf.
Livestock ChecksFinding strays and counting head across pasture, woods, and fence lines, day or night.
Lost Pet SearchA thermal search over fields, woods, and yards for a dog or other pet that has gotten out.
Deer Herd CountsOff-season counts of deer on a property for land managers and hunting clubs.
Compost & Silage Hot SpotsPiles that are heating from the inside show warm areas at the surface, an early sign of spoilage or fire risk.

Industrial

Stockpile Hot SpotsMulch, wood chip, and coal piles can self-heat. A scan shows where a pile is warming before it smolders.
Tank Level ChecksOn uninsulated tanks, the fill line often shows as a temperature change on the tank wall.
Steam & Buried Pipe LeaksLeaking steam and hot-water lines warm the ground above them, which shows from the air.

Plain Talk

What Thermal Can and Can't Do

It Reads SurfacesA thermal camera sees surface temperature. It does not see through walls, roofs, water, or a full leaf canopy.
Conditions DecideSun, wind, rain, and time of day change what shows up. Flights are timed for the best contrast, and rescheduled when conditions won't cooperate.
Findings Are LeadsA warm or cool spot is an indication to check, not a diagnosis. Every finding is delivered with a visible-light photo of the same spot.
