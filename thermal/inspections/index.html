Plain Talk

What a Thermal Scan Is Not

Thermal imaging reads surface temperature. It shows where to look, and it can miss problems that aren't producing a temperature difference on the day of the flight. Findings are indications for a qualified trade to verify. They are not a diagnosis, a warranty inspection, or a code inspection.

FAQ

Thermal Inspection Questions

What Does a Thermal Inspection Show?

Differences in surface temperature. Wet roof insulation holds heat longer than dry insulation, a failing solar cell runs hotter than its neighbors, and warm air leaking from a building shows against a cold wall. Each finding is delivered as a thermal image paired with a visible-light photo of the same spot.

When Is the Best Time to Fly?

It depends on the target. Flat roofs are scanned after sunset following a sunny day. Solar arrays are scanned in full sun while producing. Building envelopes are scanned in cold weather with the heat on. The flight is scheduled for the right window.

Does Someone Need to Be on Site?

No. With written consent, a site can be flown with nobody from the client present.

How Fast Is Delivery?

Four days or less from quote request to delivered data, weather permitting.

Is This a Roof or Electrical Inspection Report?

No. Thermal imaging shows where to look. Findings are indications for a roofer, electrician, engineer, or solar technician to verify, not a diagnosis or a code inspection.
