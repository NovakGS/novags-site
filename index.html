Thermal Imaging

A radiometric thermal camera reads surface temperature from the air, day or night, for searches and inspections that visible light can't answer.

  • Deer RecoveryA thermal search for a downed deer in corn, marsh, or timber. Recovery only, flown under Wisconsin DNR policy.
  • Roof Moisture ScansWet insulation under a flat roof holds the day's heat after sunset. A dusk flight outlines the areas for a roofer to check.
  • Solar Panel ScansHot cells, failed diodes, and strings that have dropped offline show as warm spots while the array is producing.
  • Heat Loss SurveysIn cold weather, missing insulation and air leaks show as warm patches on walls, parapets, and around openings.
  • Rooftop HVAC ChecksRooftop units scanned for parts running hotter than the rest, without sending anyone up.
  • Ice Dam & Roof Heat LossWarm streaks on a winter roof show where heat is escaping and where ice dams are likely to form.
  • Snowmelt System ChecksHeated driveways, walks, and ramps scanned to show which loops are working and where one has gone cold.
  • Drainage Tile LocatingAfter a rain, soil over a working tile line dries differently. The lines are traced from the air and put on a map.
  • Irrigation Leak ChecksWet soil runs cooler than dry soil, so leaks and overwatered zones stand out across a field or turf.
  • Livestock ChecksFinding strays and counting head across pasture, woods, and fence lines, day or night.
  • Lost Pet SearchA thermal search over fields, woods, and yards for a dog or other pet that has gotten out.
  • Deer Herd CountsOff-season counts of deer on a property for land managers and hunting clubs.
  • Compost & Silage Hot SpotsPiles that are heating from the inside show warm areas at the surface, an early sign of spoilage or fire risk.
  • Stockpile Hot SpotsMulch, wood chip, and coal piles can self-heat. A scan shows where a pile is warming before it smolders.
  • Tank Level ChecksOn uninsulated tanks, the fill line often shows as a temperature change on the tank wall.
  • Steam & Buried Pipe LeaksLeaking steam and hot-water lines warm the ground above them, which shows from the air.
