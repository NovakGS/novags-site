Flight permission terms

Last updated October 19, 2026

By granting permission, you allow Novak Geospatial Solutions (“Novak|GS”) to access the exterior of the property you listed for drone mapping flights, on these terms.

  1. Scope. Permission covers the option you chose on the permission form: single use, meaning one mapping visit, or multiple visits, meaning repeat visits as long as Novak|GS notifies you before each one. Each visit takes about one hour at a date and time agreed in advance, and Novak|GS will contact you before arriving.
  2. Operations. Flights are conducted by an FAA-certified remote pilot under 14 CFR Part 107. Novak|GS launches and lands only from a location you approve, avoids flying over people, and will postpone for weather or safety.
  3. Insurance. Novak|GS carries drone liability insurance. A certificate of insurance is available on request.
  4. Cost. There is no cost to you. Novak|GS will provide a digital copy of the aerial map, delivered as a download link, at no charge.
  5. Privacy. Flights are for mapping only. Novak|GS will not intentionally photograph people, and will blur or remove identifiable people, vehicles, or neighboring residences from any imagery shown publicly.
  6. Data. Imagery is stored on Novak|GS's secure business systems and is never sold. Novak|GS may show imagery in its portfolio and on its website, never with your business name or address, unless you opt out on the permission form.
  7. Withdrawal. You may withdraw permission at any time, before or after the flight, by calling or texting 608-820-0224 or emailing info@novakgs.com. Withdrawal after the flight also withdraws portfolio use.
  8. Duration. Permission expires 12 months from the date signed unless you renew it. Single-use permission also ends once its one visit is complete.

Questions or a change of mind?
